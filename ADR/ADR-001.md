# Architecture Decision Record - 0001

## Every event must be :
- Uniquely identifiable (for idempotency, deduplication),
- timestamped (fro ordering and temporal reasoning (might not be clear why at this stage of the project but it will once the architecture diagram will be on the readme.md))
- typed (controlled voc)
- tied to a service/entity
- structured payload and interpretable

ive also added a checksum (crc32): its not for the integrity check nor duplication nor detecting corrupted events but to enforce our real invariant: Two identical events have equal checksum , so it helps with serialization issues , duplicated events with a slight difference and transport corruption (rare but still nice to have a layer of protection against).

- since our schema might evolve from the moment im currently writing this , im adding a version field to ensure backward compatibility.
